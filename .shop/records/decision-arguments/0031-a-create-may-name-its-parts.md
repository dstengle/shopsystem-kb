# 0031 A create may name its parts

2026-10-10. A create, alone or in a set, may give any part it carries a name of the client's choosing, unique in its collection and of the plain-name grammar; a part given none is named by kb as today. So a client can link to a part of an artifact it creates in the same set. Asked for by shop-knowledge's migration, whose scenarios link to the behaviour lines they formulate. Rests on 0022. The user's decision.
