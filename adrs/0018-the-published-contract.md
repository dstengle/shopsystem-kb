# 0018 What kb publishes to its clients

2026-09-27. A client depends on kb through these alone, versioned together by kb's release tag: `kb.proto` and its generated messages (`kb.contract.kb_pb2`), the in-process client (`kb.client.connect`, with the clock a client's own tests may give it), `kb.content` (content as canonical YAML 1.2 text: `loads`, `dumps`, `text`, and `NotCanonical`, the refusal of text kb cannot keep, re-exported there), and every fault's `rule` name. A fault's message wording, the store's files and layout, and every other module are kb's own and may change behind the contract without notice. Asked by shop-knowledge, which will know nothing else of kb (shopsystem-knowledge adrs/0047). Refines 0008.
