# 0021 kb served from a container, beside its callers

2026-10-07. kb ships an image that serves a store from a container: `ENTRYPOINT ["kb"]`, the store at `/data`, served at `0.0.0.0:50051`, its default command `serve /data --listen 0.0.0.0:50051 --start`. The operator is an activity a developer takes on, and the development environment is containerized, so a laptop and a deployment have one shape: callers reach kb by its service name on a shared network (`kb:50051`), never by a port published to the host. `kb serve --start` starts a store on the first `up` when a role is named through `KB_ACTOR`, and serves the one there otherwise. Seeding belongs to `kb init --seed`, which starts and imports out of sight and puts the store in place whole, and is refused over a store that exists. A caller's connection reaches it through compose (`configs:` mounted as `kb/server.yaml` under its `KB_ROOT`), with no change to kb's discovery. The image is built from the repository at a tag first; publishing it to a registry is a later change. The user's decisions (2026-10-07). Extends 0020.
